# Scrum Ceremonies

Scrum ceremonies are regular meetings that help the team plan, execute, inspect, and improve their work.

## 1. Sprint Planning

- Conducted at the beginning of each Sprint.
- Team selects Product Backlog items.
- Defines the Sprint Goal.
- Creates the Sprint Backlog.

---

## 2. Daily Scrum

- A daily meeting lasting about 15 minutes.
- Team members discuss:
  - What was completed yesterday?
  - What will be done today?
  - Are there any blockers?

---

## 3. Sprint Review

- Conducted at the end of the Sprint.
- Demonstrates the completed product increment.
- Collects stakeholder feedback.
- Updates the Product Backlog if needed.

---

## 4. Sprint Retrospective

- Held after the Sprint Review.
- Team reflects on the Sprint.
- Identifies improvements for future Sprints.
- Discusses what went well and what can be improved.
