# Scrum Artifacts

Scrum artifacts provide transparency about the work and progress of the project.

## 1. Product Backlog

A prioritized list of all desired product features, enhancements, and bug fixes.

Managed by:

- Product Owner

---

## 2. Sprint Backlog

A subset of Product Backlog items selected for the current Sprint.

Managed by:

- Development Team

---

## 3. Increment

The Increment is the completed work delivered at the end of a Sprint.

Characteristics:

- Fully tested
- Integrated
- Potentially releasable
- Meets the Definition of Done
