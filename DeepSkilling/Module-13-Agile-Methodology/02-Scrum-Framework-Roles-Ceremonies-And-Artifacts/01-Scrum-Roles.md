# Scrum Roles

Scrum defines three primary roles that work together to deliver a successful product.

## 1. Product Owner

The Product Owner is responsible for maximizing the value of the product.

### Responsibilities

- Defines product vision
- Manages the Product Backlog
- Prioritizes backlog items
- Represents customer requirements
- Accepts completed work

---

## 2. Scrum Master

The Scrum Master ensures that the Scrum framework is understood and followed.

### Responsibilities

- Facilitates Scrum events
- Removes impediments
- Coaches the Scrum Team
- Promotes Agile practices
- Improves team productivity

---

## 3. Development Team

The Development Team consists of professionals who build and deliver the product increment.

### Responsibilities

- Design the solution
- Develop features
- Perform testing
- Fix defects
- Deliver a potentially shippable increment every Sprint

The Development Team is self-organizing and cross-functional.
