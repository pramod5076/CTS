# Overview of Agile

Agile is a software development methodology that emphasizes iterative development, collaboration, adaptability, and continuous delivery of value to customers.

Instead of completing the entire project before delivery, Agile divides the project into small iterations called **Sprints**, where each sprint delivers a working increment of the product.

## Key Characteristics

- Iterative development
- Incremental delivery
- Customer collaboration
- Continuous feedback
- Adaptability to changing requirements
- Cross-functional teams
- Frequent software releases

## Advantages

- Faster delivery of working software
- Better customer satisfaction
- Early detection of issues
- Improved product quality
- Easier adaptation to changing requirements
