# Planning Poker

Planning Poker is a consensus-based estimation technique used by Agile teams to estimate Story Points.

Each team member receives a set of cards containing Story Point values.

## Process

1. Product Owner explains the user story.
2. Team discusses the requirements.
3. Each member secretly selects a Story Point card.
4. Everyone reveals their cards simultaneously.
5. If estimates differ significantly, the team discusses the reasons.
6. Voting continues until a consensus is reached.

## Advantages

- Encourages collaboration
- Reduces estimation bias
- Improves team understanding
- Produces more accurate estimates
