# Velocity and Burndown Charts

## Velocity

Velocity is the total number of Story Points completed by a Scrum Team during a Sprint.

### Example

| Sprint | Story Points Completed |
|---------|-----------------------:|
| Sprint 1 | 25 |
| Sprint 2 | 28 |
| Sprint 3 | 30 |

Average Velocity:

```

(25 + 28 + 30) / 3 = 27.67 Story Points

```

Velocity helps teams estimate how much work they can complete in future Sprints.

---

## Burndown Chart

A Burndown Chart tracks the amount of remaining work during a Sprint.

### Benefits

- Visualizes Sprint progress
- Identifies delays early
- Helps monitor Sprint completion
- Supports project tracking

A downward trend indicates that the team is progressing toward completing the Sprint Goal.
