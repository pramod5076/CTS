# Story Points

Story Points are a relative unit used in Agile to estimate the effort required to complete a user story.

Instead of measuring time, Story Points consider:

- Complexity
- Amount of work
- Risk
- Uncertainty

## Common Story Point Scale

Many Scrum teams use the Fibonacci sequence:

```

1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34

```

Higher Story Points indicate greater effort and complexity.

## Benefits

- Encourages relative estimation
- Avoids inaccurate time-based estimates
- Improves Sprint planning
- Supports team collaboration
