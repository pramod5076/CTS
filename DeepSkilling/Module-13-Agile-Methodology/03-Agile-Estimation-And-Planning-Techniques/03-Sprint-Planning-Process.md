# Sprint Planning Process

Sprint Planning is the first event of every Sprint where the Scrum Team decides what work will be completed.

## Steps

1. Product Owner presents prioritized Product Backlog items.
2. Scrum Team discusses each item.
3. Team estimates the effort.
4. Team selects stories based on capacity.
5. Sprint Goal is defined.
6. Sprint Backlog is created.

## Output

- Sprint Goal
- Sprint Backlog
- Task breakdown for implementation

Sprint Planning ensures the team has a clear plan before development begins.
