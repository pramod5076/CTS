# Generative AI vs Traditional AI

| Generative AI | Traditional (Discriminative) AI |
|---------------|---------------------------------|
| Creates new content | Classifies or predicts data |
| Learns patterns to generate outputs | Learns patterns to recognize inputs |
| Produces text, images, code, audio | Predicts labels or categories |
| Used for chatbots, content creation, coding | Used for spam detection, fraud detection, image classification |
| Focuses on creativity | Focuses on prediction and decision-making |

## Summary

Traditional AI answers **"What is this?"**, whereas Generative AI answers **"Create something new based on what you've learned."**
