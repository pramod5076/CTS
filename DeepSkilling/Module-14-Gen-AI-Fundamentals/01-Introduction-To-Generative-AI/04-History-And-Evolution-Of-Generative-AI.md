# History and Evolution of Generative AI

Generative AI has evolved significantly over the past several decades.

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1960s | Early rule-based chatbots such as ELIZA demonstrated basic human-computer conversations. |
| 2014 | Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) introduced realistic image generation. |
| 2020 | GPT-3 showcased advanced natural language generation using Large Language Models. |
| 2022 | ChatGPT brought conversational AI to millions of users worldwide. |
| 2022 onwards | GitHub Copilot, image generators, and multimodal AI systems accelerated AI-assisted development and content creation. |

## Evolution Summary

- Rule-based chatbots
- Deep learning models
- GANs
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Modern multimodal Generative AI systems
